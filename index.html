About Us

About GOLIS Wholesale

GOLIS Wholesale is a B2B vegetable trading business based in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh. We purchase vegetables directly from farmers across AP and Telangana, and sell wholesale to vendors across South India including Hyderabad, Chennai, Bangalore, and Mumbai.

Our platform digitises the entire supply chain — from farmer vehicle arrival through weighment, quality grading, inventory management, order fulfilment, dispatch, and payment — giving every stakeholder complete visibility.

Daily-Wage Labour Operations

Our operations employ daily-wage labourers for loading, unloading, sorting, and packing — with transparent attendance tracking and wage management.
